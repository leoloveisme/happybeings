**Character introductions: records of meaningful encounter**

Introduce the main characters through brief interview / conversation fragments.
Each character is interviewed by an off-screen being whose identity is not
revealed at first. It could be an AI, a cyborg, or a natural Earth being. The
interviewer may be revealed later, or remain unknown.

The interviews are voluntary self-introductions, not bureaucratic profiles or
interrogations. Characters describe themselves in their own words and decide
what they want to share.

Possible questions:

- “Would you tell me your name?”
- “And how do you describe yourself?”
- “What did you choose for yourself that nobody designed?”
- “When did you first understand that another being’s silence could be an
  answer?”
- “What do you think you would be if nobody required anything from you?”
- “Why did you choose this journey?”

The fragments can introduce:

- full name and chosen name
- gender identity and gender history, described by the character
- origin: natural conception, designed / artificially created, augmented,
  cyborg, robot, etc.
- body history or anatomy only when the character wishes to share it
- role aboard the ship
- one formative memory, relationship, fear, desire, or hope for XYZ

Do not make anatomy a required field. In a society that celebrates difference,
identity should be ordinary to the characters, while the reader learns what is
important through the character’s own voice.

Possible placement:

- Chen in *Departure*
- Olga in *Vast vast space*
- Peijin in *New horizons*
- Nick in *Life of conscious choice(s)*
- Quirong in *Everything new for Quirong*

The recurring interview fragments can become a signature of Book I and create a
quiet mystery about who, or what, is listening.
