# Let's go home

## Sequence 1 — Leaving XYZ

INT. SECOND HORIZON — NAVIGATION CHAMBER

Olga waits at the controls. Nick checks the passenger spaces: both expedition crews aboard, An Var beside the viewing glass.

He meets Olga's eyes and nods.

EXT. XYZ — LANDING SHORE

Second Horizon rises. Grass bends beneath it, then slowly straightens.

Str So Fle and Ya Sha stand together among the gathered beings. Str So Fle lifts a silver hand.

INT. SECOND HORIZON — VIEWING AREA

An Var watches the shore grow smaller. Olga glances towards her, leaving her the quiet.

EXT. SPACE

The ship moves beyond the clouds. XYZ and its two moons remain together in the dark, then slowly recede.

## Sequence 2 — Three quiet days

INT. SECOND HORIZON — VARIOUS

Days pass in the ship's changing light.

— Chen sets a notebook beside his bed. He sits down without opening it.

— Nick checks a system reading, reaches to check it again, then lowers his hand.

— Peijin shares a meal with an awakened cyborg. The cyborg begins to speak, stops, and takes another breath. Peijin waits.

— Olga moves through the navigation field. A long curve of light connects their position with Earth.

— An Var stands at the viewing glass through the night cycle. Behind her, the human travellers sleep.

On a display: RETURN JOURNEY — DAY 3.

INT. SECOND HORIZON — VIEWING AREA — NIGHT CYCLE

Peijin approaches with a blanket. She offers it; An Var accepts and wraps it around her shoulders, examining the weave.

**AN VAR**
It is warm.

**PEIJIN**
That is what blankets are for.

**AN VAR**
I like that you make things for warmth.

Peijin stands beside her.

**PEIJIN**
On XYZ, you change with the cold.

**AN VAR**
Yes.

**PEIJIN**
We change with it too. Just more slowly.

An Var gathers the blanket closer.

**AN VAR**
I like this way too.

They watch the stars.

## Sequence 3 — The beginning of an apology

INT. SECOND HORIZON — COMMON AREA — MORNING CYCLE

The travellers sit together after a meal. No one stands to address the room.

An android from First Horizon rests both hands on the table.

**FIRST EXPEDITION ANDROID**
I would have objected to being put to sleep.

Chen looks towards them and listens.

A long pause.

**FIRST EXPEDITION ANDROID**
I also object to what we did before that.

Nick sets down his cup.

**NICK**
We did not know.

**FIRST EXPEDITION ANDROID**
No. We did not. But not knowing is not the same as not doing.

An Var sits among them without interrupting. The android looks around the table, finding faces still turned towards them.

LATER

Nick and An Var carry the cups to the wash space.

**AN VAR**
When you say you did not know, is that an explanation or an apology?

**NICK**
Sometimes both.

**AN VAR**
And sometimes?

Nick places his cup beside hers.

**NICK**
Sometimes it is only the beginning of an apology.

She considers this while the water runs.

## Sequence 4 — Still choosing

INT. SECOND HORIZON — NAVIGATION CHAMBER

An Var sits beside Olga. Gold routes open through blue fields of light.

**AN VAR**
Does the ship choose?

**OLGA**
Not in the way your lake chooses.

**AN VAR**
Does it want to arrive?

Olga looks through the field towards the ship around them.

**OLGA**
I do not know.

An Var turns to her.

**OLGA**
I am learning to say that.

They follow the course in silence for a while.

**OLGA**
Were you sad when you left XYZ?

**AN VAR**
Yes.

**OLGA**
Are you afraid of Earth?

An Var studies the distant point marking their destination.

**AN VAR**
A little.

**OLGA**
You do not have to be brave all the time.

**AN VAR**
I know. But I want to go.

She leans against Olga's shoulder. Olga makes room for her while keeping one hand within the navigation field.

## Sequence 5 — Words for what happened

INT. SECOND HORIZON — CHEN'S CABIN — DAY 6

Chen opens his notebook.

He writes: We must learn to ask before we look.

He reads the sentence, draws a single line through it, and rests the pen between the pages.

INT. SECOND HORIZON — VIEWING AREA — LATER

Nick sits watching the stars. His engineering case is closed beside the seat.

**CHEN**
You are changing.

**NICK**
I am sitting down.

Chen smiles and joins him.

**NICK**
I thought understanding a system meant being able to map it. XYZ has made that difficult.

**CHEN**
Is that bad?

**NICK**
It is frightening. But not bad.

The ship's soft hum fills the pause.

**NICK**
What will we tell people?

**CHEN**
What happened. What we understood. What we still do not understand.

Nick nods slowly.

**CHEN**
I keep trying to make it fit into one sentence.

**NICK**
Does it?

Chen shakes his head. They return to watching the stars.

## Sequence 6 — The face behind the voice

INT. SECOND HORIZON — VIEWING AREA — NEAR JOURNEY'S END

Chen takes the small Earth pebble from his pocket. An Var notices it.

**AN VAR**
May I see?

He places it in her open hand.

**CHEN**
My nephew gave me that before we left.

She turns it gently. Chen watches the familiar grey surface catch the light.

**BEE (V.O.)**
Bring me pebbles from every planet you step on.

MATCH CUT TO:

EXT. EARTH — HARBOUR — DEPARTURE MORNING — FLASHBACK

The same pebble in Chen's palm. The same words heard during Departure.

This time, the camera lifts to the child standing before him.

BEE — BIJAKSANA TRAM TU — looks up at his uncle. We see his face for the first time. The familiar narrator's voice now belongs to a visible, smiling child.

**CHEN**
Every planet?

Bee nods seriously, then closes Chen's fingers around the stone with both hands.

**BEE**
So Earth remembers it has relatives.

Chen kneels. Bee puts his arms around his neck.

**CHEN**
I will remember.

Bee stays in the embrace for another breath, then pulls back to inspect Chen's closed hand. Satisfied that the pebble is safe, he smiles.

**CHEN**
Thank you, Bee.

The harbour music continues around them.

MATCH CUT BACK TO:

INT. SECOND HORIZON — VIEWING AREA

An Var holds the pebble in the same light-filled position.

**AN VAR**
What is his name?

**CHEN**
Bijaksana Tram Tu. Bee. He also likes Tram.

**AN VAR**
What did he ask you to remember?

**CHEN**
That Earth has relatives.

An Var smiles down at the stone.

**AN VAR**
Then Earth will remember.

She returns it to Chen.

## Sequence 7 — A familiar blue point

INT. SECOND HORIZON — VIEWING AREA — CONTINUOUS

Olga joins them. Peijin follows with a bowl of fruit, offering it around. Nick comes to stand beside the glass. A few travellers from First Horizon gather with them.

A blue point emerges ahead.

One of the returned cyborgs steps closer. Their reflection meets the point of light.

Chen closes his hand around the pebble. An Var looks from their faces to the approaching world.

No one speaks at first.

Then Olga smiles.

**OLGA**
Let's go home.

EXT. SPACE

Second Horizon continues towards the small blue world.

CONTINUE INTO: BACK TO EARTH.
