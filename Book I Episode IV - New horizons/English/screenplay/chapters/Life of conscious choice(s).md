# Life of conscious choice(s)

## Sequence 1 — A system that tells the truth

INT. HOUSE OF MEANINGFUL ENCOUNTERS — BEFORE DEPARTURE

Nick sits with his hands resting on his knees. The interviewer is outside the frame.

**INTERVIEWER (O.S.)**
Would you tell me your name?

**NICK**
Nick Amalia Pantonelli-Bergmann. I build things. I repair things. I like a system that tells the truth about how it works.

**INTERVIEWER (O.S.)**
And how do you describe yourself?

**NICK**
I am intersex. I was naturally conceived and naturally born. I was born with female genitalia. That is not a tragedy. It was simply my beginning.

**INTERVIEWER (O.S.)**
What do you expect to find at XYZ?

**NICK**
A system I cannot diagram. I dislike that already.

A small smile.

**INTERVIEWER (O.S.)**
And what do you hope to find?

**NICK**
That it is real.

CUT TO:

## Sequence 2 — Learning to listen

EXT. XYZ — FOREST PATH — EVENING

An Var approaches a curtain of leaves. The branches ease apart, opening a path for the crew.

Nick pauses at the opening, looking for what moved them. Quirong waits for him. They continue together.

Blue moss cushions the roots of pale trees. Insects click beneath the leaves. Wind carries a low note through the trunks.

A bird calls three times. The clicking rhythm changes.

**AN VAR**
There is always something going on. It depends where you put your attention.

Peijin stops beside white mushrooms. Her hand hovers above them without touching.

**PEIJIN**
They are singing.

An Var smiles.

**PEIJIN**
Very quietly.

**AN VAR**
They have no reason to be loud.

Nick crouches beside Peijin. Let the surrounding sounds soften as a delicate, layered vibration becomes distinct.

**NICK**
Everything is communicating?

**AN VAR**
Trees, mushrooms, birds, bugs. Stones. Water. You call some things silent because you do not yet know how they speak.

## Sequence 3 — The lake offers

EXT. XYZ — LAKE — EVENING

A lake rests inside a bowl of silver-grey rock. The blue moon lies reflected in its surface.

Nick approaches the edge. His reflection gives way to roots beneath soil, fish below ice, birds crossing an unfamiliar sky.

He steps back.

**NICK**
I did not ask to see that.

The images disappear. Only water remains.

**AN VAR**
The lake offered because you were looking. It should have waited. I am sorry.

**NICK**
The lake can decide?

**AN VAR**
Everything can decide.

Nick looks at the water again, staying where he is.

**AN VAR**
Our experiences enter the conscious energy space. When you want to understand, you can ask.

**NICK**
Who maintains it?

**AN VAR**
Everyone.

**NICK**
That is not an answer.

**AN VAR**
It is the answer.

Olga sits on the rock, leaving a little distance from the edge.

**OLGA**
On Earth, we have houses, records, devices. People who care for them.

**AN VAR**
You have made so many ways to help one another remember.

## Sequence 4 — Falling upward

The lake stirs.

A shining column rises. Beneath it, a figure moves upwards through the water, turns in the air, and lands on the shore.

Droplets hang for a moment before falling around YA SHA. Her dark hair streams down her back; green-gold colours move across her shoulders. She is laughing.

Quirong has stepped forward, hands raised to catch her.

She notices and softens her laughter.

**YA SHA**
You thought I was falling.

**QUIRONG**
I did.

**YA SHA**
I was. Just not in the direction you expected.

A small, rabbitlike being emerges from behind a nearby root. Ya Sha crouches and opens her hands. He climbs into them.

**AN VAR**
This is Ya Sha.

**YA SHA**
And this is my brother, Kho Pa.

Olga leans closer, smiling. Kho Pa turns towards her voice as she quietly repeats his name.

Ya Sha settles him beside her and turns back to Quirong, opening her arms.

**YA SHA**
May I?

**QUIRONG**
Yes.

They embrace. She draws back with a surprised smile.

**YA SHA**
You are not soft. I like soft.

She gently squeezes her own forearm, then strokes Kho Pa's back.

**QUIRONG**
I am soft inside.

She laughs. Quirong lowers its hands slowly.

**YA SHA**
You amuse me.

**QUIRONG**
What are you doing here?

**YA SHA**
We are experiencing. Do you want to experience with us?

**QUIRONG**
What do I have to do?

**YA SHA**
Nothing. Just be with us.

**QUIRONG**
I can do that. I am Quirong.

**YA SHA**
I am happy you came, Quirong.

Quirong sits beside her. Kho Pa noses its still hand.

## Sequence 5 — Enjoying being yourself

The crew settles around the lake. Nick places his case behind him.

**YA SHA**
Do you enjoy being you?

Nick looks up. She is asking him.

**NICK**
Yes. I enjoy being me as I am now.

He glances at Peijin.

**PEIJIN**
I enjoy being me, too. Not because I have never changed. Because I have.

**NICK**
We were both naturally conceived. Our genes were not designed before we were born. We can change our appearance now, if we choose. We have technologies for that.

**YA SHA**
And if you choose to stay as you are?

**NICK**
Then we do. We celebrate uniqueness. Similarity. Being alive.

**PEIJIN**
We celebrate.

Ya Sha raises one hand. Her fingertips deepen from green to blue and back again.

**YA SHA**
Some of us change often. Some choose one form for a whole life. We can be trees, fish, birds, water. Or remain as we are.

Kho Pa curls beside her. Quirong watches her changing hand, then opens its own.

**OLGA**
And travelling? Do you use ships?

**YA SHA**
We see where we want to go, and we go there. Here, or on another planet. Mostly, we have everything we need here.

Olga looks across the lake, absorbing this.

## Sequence 6 — Where is Kho Pa?

EXT. XYZ — LAKE — A LATER DAY

Daylight on the same rock. Ya Sha sits by the water. Quirong joins her readily; the others follow.

Nick looks towards the roots where they first saw Kho Pa.

**NICK**
Where is your brother? We wanted to see him again.

Ya Sha opens her arms to the lake, trees, sky, and the space between them.

**YA SHA**
He is here.

**OLGA**
Where?

**YA SHA**
Everywhere. Since you met him, he has chosen another form.

**PEIJIN**
Did he die?

**YA SHA**
No. He changed. He wanted to experience being unbounded.

She traces the dust on the stone. It rises into a small mountain ridge.

**YA SHA**
To make a mountain, many beings join together. When one moves away, the others feel the change. The same with a sea. Our planet is alive with all of us.

The ridge relaxes into dust.

**CHEN**
Can he still think? Know we are here?

**YA SHA**
Of course.

**NICK**
Can he hear us?

**YA SHA**
He can perceive you. But he will not enter your consciousness without permission. That would be violence, even if he meant no harm.

Nick looks at the lake. It reflects only the sky.

## Sequence 7 — Permission

**OLGA**
I would like to speak with him. How do I give permission?

**YA SHA**
Tell him. He can hear you.

Olga faces the open air.

**OLGA**
Kho Pa, you may enter my consciousness. I want to hear you.

Hold on Olga. Her breath catches. Tears gather, then she laughs once and covers her mouth.

**CHEN**
Olga?

**OLGA**
He is saying hello. He is not only everywhere. He is happy everywhere.

Peijin steps forward.

**PEIJIN**
Kho Pa, I give you permission. Please speak with me.

She remains still, eyes open. Her hands slowly unfold.

Through her hearing, the forest's separate sounds gather into a living whole. Water beneath stone. Wings above leaves. The rhythm of roots and wind.

**PEIJIN**
I can feel the roots. The birds. The water under the stone. I can feel him being all of it.

An Var waits until Peijin looks towards her.

**AN VAR**
May I share something too?

**PEIJIN**
Yes.

A quiet moment passes between them.

**PEIJIN**
I heard you.

She laughs through tears.

**AN VAR**
Kho Pa made the space wider for you. Only for a little while.

Nick takes a step, then stops. Ya Sha gives him time.

**NICK**
How long can I take to decide?

**YA SHA**
All the time you need. All the time of eternity.

Nick sits beside his unopened case. He listens.

Quirong remains beside Ya Sha, its hand resting where Kho Pa had touched it.

The lake holds the trees and the sky.

CONTINUE INTO: BEAUTIFUL NEW WORLD.
