# Enjoying being

<!-- Adaptation direction: Col An and Sai Ya are adults, in XYZ's adulthood phase of being and enjoyment. This chapter belongs entirely to them. No Earth crew, spectators, spoken dialogue, or voice-over. Their history is expressed through their ease together, without explanatory titles. -->

## Sequence 1 — The evening opens

EXT. XYZ — SHORE — EVENING

The orange moon rises beyond the headland. The blue moon is already high.

A small wave travels up the sand, slows, and returns. Its thin edge catches both colours.

Wind moves through the trees above the beach. Leaves turn in a long, travelling ripple. A loose strand of pale hair lifts with the same current.

SAI YA turns her face into the wind.

Beside her stands COL AN. Both have adult humanlike forms and wear no clothes. His hair is dark and close-cropped; fine blue lines move beneath the warm brown skin of his arms. Her pale hair falls loose, and living leaflike patterns curl over her arms and shoulders.

She looks towards the open beach. He catches the look.

Sai Ya takes one quick step, then another.

Col An is already moving beside her.

## Sequence 2 — Running together

EXT. XYZ — WATER'S EDGE — CONTINUOUS

They run along the firm, wet sand.

Their footfalls make a changing rhythm beneath the surf. Sai Ya curves towards the water. Col An follows until she turns suddenly inland, laughing.

He tries to follow the turn, slips, catches himself with one hand, and springs up again. She doubles back to him. He reaches for her; she lets him catch her hand.

They turn together, arms outstretched, gathering speed until both lose their footing and fall into the sand.

A moment of stillness.

Then laughter. Col An lifts his head, sand caught in his hair. Sai Ya brushes it away, only to scatter more across his forehead.

He closes his eyes and lets her try again.

She kisses him. He smiles against her cheek.

A wave reaches their feet. Both look down.

Sai Ya is up first. She holds out her hand.

They run straight into the sea.

## Sequence 3 — The sea receives them

EXT. XYZ — BAY — CONTINUOUS

Water rises around their legs. Their strides shorten, splash, become swimming.

They dive beneath a small wave together.

UNDERWATER

The surface trembles overhead, blue and amber. Bubbles rise between them. Their hands meet briefly as they glide past one another.

Sai Ya turns towards the light. Col An follows.

AT THE SURFACE

They emerge several lengths apart. She laughs; he answers with a laugh of his own.

They swim towards each other, pass, circle, and return. Keep the movement spacious enough to feel the water carrying them between strokes.

Sai Ya rolls onto her back. Col An floats beside her.

For a while, neither moves except with the swell.

Above them, two moons. Around them, two broken paths of light.

The wind reaches the bay. The surface dimples, and the reflections scatter.

## Sequence 4 — Colours answering

EXT. XYZ — BAY — LATER

The orange moon deepens slowly towards red.

Its reflection warms across Sai Ya's face. Gold appears within the patterns at her wrists and travels towards her shoulders.

Col An lifts his hand from the water. The blue beneath his skin shades into orange along his fingers.

He turns his hand. Sai Ya brings hers alongside it. Drops fall from their fingertips in alternating colours.

They watch, delighted, then look up together.

A green tint spreads across the higher moon. Its path on the water changes with it.

Their colours shift again: blue beside gold, orange edged with green. Let the changes unfold across sky, water, and skin within the same unhurried shots.

Sai Ya sweeps a hand across the surface. A fan of bright drops passes between them. Col An ducks too late.

Her laughter breaks the quiet. His follows.

<!-- Moon continuity: this evening uses blue moving towards green and orange towards red. The established brown and yellow variations remain available for other evenings. -->

## Sequence 5 — Nearness

EXT. XYZ — SHALLOWS — LATER

They drift back towards the sheltered shallows.

Sai Ya reaches for Col An. He comes closer. Their foreheads touch.

She wipes a drop of water from his eyebrow. He rests his hand against hers. They kiss and hold one another as the water rises and falls around them.

Remain with their faces for a quiet moment, then widen to the moonlit bay.

DISSOLVE TO:

Moonlight on an empty stretch of wet sand. Another wave crosses it.

## Sequence 6 — And again

EXT. XYZ — SHORE — LATER

Col An and Sai Ya rest together at the edge of the sea. Water reaches their feet and withdraws.

She lies with her head against his shoulder. His fingers rest loosely beside hers in the sand.

The wind moves through the trees. Their leaves carry a layered sound, almost a song, beneath the steady surf.

Hold this stillness. The moons have moved a little farther across the sky.

Sai Ya lifts her head.

Col An opens his eyes. He sees her looking down the beach and begins to smile.

She rises. This time, he is the first to run.

She follows, laughing, and catches his hand as they reach the shining sand.

Stay wide as they move along the shore, separating and coming together again beneath the changing light.

Their footprints fill slowly with water.

FADE OUT.
