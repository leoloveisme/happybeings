# Back to Earth

Music reference: Beautiful New World / Home Sweet Home — Danny Elfman.

## Sequence 1 — Home, seen again

EXT. SPACE — EARTH APPROACH

The blue point grows into ocean, cloud, and the familiar curve of Earth.

SECOND HORIZON approaches. Give the world time to fill the frame.

INT. SECOND HORIZON — VIEWING AREA

Chen stands beside An Var. Other travellers gather behind them, quiet as the
continents become visible.

An Var follows a weather system moving across the sea.

**AN VAR** It is beautiful.

**CHEN** It is.

She places a palm against the glass.

**AN VAR** I can feel how hard you are all trying.

Chen looks at her, then back at Earth. He remains beside her without answering.

INT. SECOND HORIZON — NAVIGATION CHAMBER

Olga guides the approach. Nick checks the landing confirmation beside her.

Through the clouds, the harbour city opens in broad circles across the water.
Gardens, workshops, homes, and quiet transit lines. The same city they left.

Peijin offers An Var her hand. An Var takes it as the ship descends.

## Sequence 2 — You came back

EXT. EARTH — HARBOUR — AFTERNOON

Second Horizon settles. The engines quieten.

A wide gathering waits beyond the landing area. People leave the approach to
the ramp clear.

INT. SECOND HORIZON — HATCH

The ramp opens. Warm air moves inside.

Chen steps into the light and stops.

EXT. EARTH — HARBOUR — CONTINUOUS

Bee stands at the front of the gathering with Chen's nieces. His hands are
clasped beneath his chin.

He sees Chen and breaks into a run.

Chen reaches the foot of the ramp, kneels, and catches him.

**BEE** You came back.

**CHEN** I did.

Bee holds on. Chen closes his eyes for a moment.

The nieces join them. Chen rises into their welcome, answering their embraces
and overlapping greetings. Stay long enough for each to be present in the
reunion.

Bee looks past Chen towards An Var, who has stopped beside Olga.

**BEE** Is this someone from the other planet?

An Var steps closer.

**AN VAR** Yes. I am An Var.

**BEE** My name is Bee. You can also call me Tram — whichever is better for you.

**AN VAR** Hello, Bee.

He smiles at the sound of his name.

## Sequence 3 — The pebble comes home

**AN VAR** Chen told me about your pebble.

Bee turns quickly to his uncle.

**BEE** Did you keep it?

Chen takes the grey stone from his pocket and places it in Bee's hand.

**CHEN** All the way there. All the way back.

Bee examines it, then looks at An Var.

**BEE** Did you see it on your planet?

**AN VAR** On the ship. He told me Earth should remember it has relatives.

Bee looks from her to Chen. His fingers close around the stone.

Chen rests a hand on his shoulder. Together, they turn towards the ramp.

## Sequence 4 — Coming back together

An android from First Horizon appears in the doorway.

Someone in the gathering takes a sharp breath. The android pauses, recognising
them, then descends.

They meet halfway across the open space. Hands reach, hesitate, then hold.

Others follow: robots, androids, cyborgs. Names are called. One returning being
stands still while a friend presses their forehead against theirs. Another
laughs before they can speak.

Peijin waits beside a traveller who needs more time. Olga greets someone with
both arms. Nick sets his case down so he can return an embrace.

An Var watches with Bee and Chen's nieces nearby. Around her, curious glances
become smiles and quiet greetings. She carries no luggage; the blue and gold of
her chosen form catch the afternoon light.

Peijin joins her when the last of the first crew has reached their waiting
friends.

**AN VAR** Your people are very good at coming back together.

**PEIJIN** We have had a lot of practice.

**AN VAR** That can be sad.

**PEIJIN** Sometimes.

**AN VAR** And beautiful?

Peijin looks across the harbour.

**PEIJIN** Yes, and beautiful.

## Sequence 5 — A place for resting

INT. HOUSE OF MEANINGFUL ENCOUNTERS — EARLY EVENING

Food passes around a table. A returning traveller leans back while someone
listens beside them. Through an open doorway, another has fallen asleep.

Chen sits among family and friends. Bee sets the pebble on the table between
them.

INT. AN VAR'S ROOM — CONTINUOUS

Olga opens the door to a room facing the sea.

An Var enters slowly. She touches the woven wall covering, studies a lamp, and
opens a book. She turns one page, then another.

The bed draws her attention. She presses a hand into the cover and watches it
spring back.

**AN VAR** You sleep here?

**OLGA** In beds like this. This one is here for you, if you want it.

**AN VAR** Every night?

**OLGA** Most nights.

An Var sits on the edge, testing it with careful delight.

**AN VAR** You have made a whole place for not being awake.

Olga laughs.

**OLGA** That is one way to describe it.

An Var looks towards the open window. Outside, a transit line curves between
gardens.

Olga sits beside her. For a while, they watch the city.

## Sequence 6 — An Var's meaningful encounter

INT. HOUSE OF MEANINGFUL ENCOUNTERS — EVENING

An Var sits at a small table. A warm recording light glows. The interviewer
remains unseen, as in the crew's earlier encounters.

**INTERVIEWER (O.S.)** Would you tell me your name?

**AN VAR** An Var.

**INTERVIEWER (O.S.)** And how do you describe yourself?

**AN VAR** I am two hundred years old. I am finishing childhood. I am going to
try youth now.

**INTERVIEWER (O.S.)** Where have you arrived?

**AN VAR** Earth.

**INTERVIEWER (O.S.)** What do you see here?

**AN VAR** So many beautiful beings. So many ways of helping. So many things that
still make you tired.

**INTERVIEWER (O.S.)** Are you afraid?

An Var thinks before answering.

**AN VAR** A little. But I am also curious. Curiosity is bigger today.

**INTERVIEWER (O.S.)** What do you hope to do on Earth?

**AN VAR** Be here. Listen. Learn. Play. Ask questions. Help if I am invited. And
make friends who do not yet know they are my sisters and brothers.

**INTERVIEWER (O.S.)** What would you like Earth to know about XYZ?

**AN VAR** That we are not somewhere you need to become. We are somewhere you can
remember. // Not sure about that place, need to thinks and brainstorm more. 

The recording light fades.

## Sequence 7 — One moon

EXT. HOUSE OF MEANINGFUL ENCOUNTERS — BALCONY — NIGHT

Earth's moon rises above the sea, alone in the sky.

An Var stands beneath it, listening. Leave room for the distant city, the
water, and a faint sustained sound within them.

Chen steps onto the balcony. She makes room beside her.

Below, light shines from workshops and common rooms. Someone closes a window
for the night. Two neighbours pause on a bridge to talk. Farther away, a small
group moves through a choreoyoga dance.

Chen watches the city with An Var.

**CHEN** Life here is pleasant. Amazing, even.

He looks up at the moon.

**CHEN** After XYZ, I understand we still have a long way to go.

An Var remains beside him. From inside comes Bee's laugh. Both turn towards it.

INT. HOUSE OF MEANINGFUL ENCOUNTERS — CONTINUOUS

Bee sits with Chen's nieces around the table. He has made a little space beside
him and waves An Var over.

She joins them. Bee slides the pebble into the centre where everyone can see it.

Chen pauses in the doorway, smiling, then comes to sit with them.

EXT. EARTH — HARBOUR CITY — NIGHT

The open window becomes one warm light among many. The city curves across the
water beneath its single moon.

FADE OUT.

END OF EPISODE IV — NEW HORIZONS.
