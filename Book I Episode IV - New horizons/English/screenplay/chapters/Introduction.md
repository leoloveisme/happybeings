**BEE (V.O.) — CRAWL TEXT**

On Earth, we stopped using money so long ago that most of us have forgotten it.

I saw some once, in a history lesson. Little metal circles. Pieces of paper.
Numbers on a screen. It was strange to think you might need those things before
someone would give you food.

Now we grow things, build things, and share what we know. We look after each
other because we care. There are no wars anymore.

We still have things to learn. Sometimes it takes a lot of talking to
understand one another.

Our cities float on the sea. Our ships travel between planets. Humans, robots,
androids, and cyborgs live and explore together. There are so many ways to be
someone.

Years ago, a ship called First Horizon reached a planet we call XYZ. Its crew
sent word of breathable air, oceans, and two moons.

Then their messages stopped. The ship disappeared.

There were friends on that ship.

Now Second Horizon is going to look for them.

**END CRAWL**

The last words drift out of sight. The music softens.
