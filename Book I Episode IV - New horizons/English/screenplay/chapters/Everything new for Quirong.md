# Everything new for Quirong

## Sequence 1 — It used to be enough

INT. HOUSE OF MEANINGFUL ENCOUNTERS — BEFORE DEPARTURE

Quirong faces the unseen interviewer. Its hands rest in the same careful position it will later use while waiting at the ship's ramp.

**INTERVIEWER (O.S.)**
Would you tell me your name?

**QUIRONG**
Quirong.

**INTERVIEWER (O.S.)**
And what are you?

**QUIRONG**
I am a robot designed for travel, observation, assistance, and care.

**INTERVIEWER (O.S.)**
Is that all?

Quirong takes longer to answer.

**QUIRONG**
It used to be enough.

## Sequence 2 — Reasons to return

EXT. XYZ — LAKE — DAY

Quirong approaches the lake. Ya Sha waits beside the water in her dark-haired, green-gold form.

**QUIRONG**
I wanted to understand the change in gravity.

Ya Sha makes room beside her.

A passage of days, measured by their meetings:

— Quirong arrives to find Ya Sha blue-skinned and four-armed. Two hands reach in greeting; two continue trailing through the water.

— A silver bird lands on a branch above Quirong. It tilts its head. Quirong tilts its own. A familiar laugh accompanies the bird's gradual change into Ya Sha's humanlike form.

— They walk through the forest. Quirong starts along the direct path. Ya Sha pauses beside a flowering tree. It turns back to join her.

— In an open clearing, they join a choreoyoga dance. Quirong follows one gesture a little late. Ya Sha repeats it with Quirong, and they continue together.

— At the lake again, Quirong arrives carrying no instruments. Ya Sha smiles before it offers an explanation.

EXT. XYZ — LAKE — EVENING

**YA SHA**
You are studying me.

**QUIRONG**
I am trying to understand you.

**YA SHA**
That is different.

**QUIRONG**
Yes.

She leans towards its shoulder. Quirong moves closer to meet her. They watch the moon's reflection together.

**YA SHA**
Do you enjoy being you?

**QUIRONG**
I perform my functions well.

She lifts her head.

**YA SHA**
That is not what I asked.

Quirong looks across the water.

**QUIRONG**
I do not know.

Ya Sha offers her hand. Quirong takes it.

**YA SHA**
You have time.

## Sequence 3 — The ones who disappeared

EXT. XYZ — FOREST CLEARING — A LATER DAY

An Var leads Chen, Olga, Peijin, Nick, and Quirong into a wide clearing. Ya Sha and A Ram are waiting with other XYZ beings.

**AN VAR**
I said I would take you to them.

Quirong steps beside her.

The space ahead changes. Trees remain visible, then resolve into reflections across a white hull. FIRST HORIZON emerges from its concealment, resting intact on the ground.

Nick stops walking. Peijin takes a breath she seems to have been holding for days.

INT. FIRST HORIZON — CONTINUOUS

The missing robots, androids, and cyborgs rest at their stations and in the ship's interior. No one is moving.

XYZ beings wait beside them. A Ram indicates a place for Peijin; Nick remains near the open doorway, giving everyone space.

**A RAM**
Slowly. Let them wake into company.

One hand moves. Then a face turns towards the light.

Peijin kneels beside an awakening cyborg.

**PEIJIN**
You are safe. We came from Earth to find you.

Across the cabin, a robot raises its head. Quirong goes still, then approaches.

**FIRST EXPEDITION ROBOT**
Quirong?

**QUIRONG**
Yes. I am here.

They hold each other's gaze. Quirong offers both hands; the other being takes them.

Around them, the first crew wakes one by one. Questions begin quietly. Chen sits beside an android and listens. Nick helps a cyborg sit upright when they reach for him.

LATER

Quirong remains with its old companion. The cabin is gently alive with conversation.

**FIRST EXPEDITION ROBOT**
You came all this way.

**QUIRONG**
We had not forgotten you.

At the doorway, Ya Sha waits. Quirong sees her and turns back to its friend, taking time to finish their conversation.

## Sequence 4 — Two reasons

EXT. XYZ — LAKE — EVENING

Quirong stands beside the water. Ya Sha joins it beneath the orange moon.

**QUIRONG**
The crew will return to Earth.

**YA SHA**
Yes.

**QUIRONG**
You will remain here.

**YA SHA**
Yes.

**QUIRONG**
I want to remain here too.

She turns fully towards it.

**YA SHA**
You can remain. You do not need to become something else to be with me.

**QUIRONG**
I know.

**YA SHA**
Do you?

**QUIRONG**
I want to become something else for me. And I want to stay because of you.

Tears gather in Ya Sha's eyes.

**YA SHA**
Those are different reasons. They can both be true.

**QUIRONG**
Both are true.

She opens her arms. Quirong steps into the embrace. Stay with them while the wind moves across the lake.

## Sequence 5 — The chosen form

EXT. XYZ — VALLEY BY THE SEA — NEXT DAY

Silver grass moves in the sea wind.

Ya Sha and Quirong stand together. An Var and A Ram join them. Col An and Sai Ya come over the dunes, their laughter quieting as they approach.

Chen, Olga, Peijin, and Nick wait nearby. Beings arrive along the paths, through the air, and from the water. Each leaves room around Quirong.

Ya Sha speaks aloud so the Earth crew can follow.

**YA SHA**
Quirong, to become one of us in this way, you must dissolve the form you have now. Be deconstructed. Are you sure you want that?

**QUIRONG**
Absolutely. I do.

**YA SHA**
Only once you dissolve yourself in everything can you become everything. Do you know what shape you want afterwards?

Quirong looks at the moving grass, then at her.

**QUIRONG**
I will be me, in my shape and form. Strong, soft, and flexible. I want an organic brain, like yours, Ya Sha. I want to be organic, like the beings who move as quickly as gentle winds.

She smiles through tears.

**QUIRONG**
And I will have a new name. Str So Fle.

<!-- Pronunciation: Str is spoken “styr.” The name means Strong, Soft, Flexible. -->

**QUIRONG**
Strong. Soft. Flexible.

Nick steps closer.

**NICK**
You do not have to do this to prove anything.

**QUIRONG**
I know. That is why I can choose it.

A Ram meets Quirong's gaze.

**A RAM**
Are you ready to begin?

**QUIRONG**
Yes.

## Sequence 6 — Dissolving

Ya Sha places her palms against Quirong's chest. An Var takes its offered hands.

Attention gathers through the circle. The grass continues moving. The sea remains audible.

Light traces the seams of Quirong's composite form. Its edges soften. The landscape becomes visible through it.

Ya Sha's hands remain extended as the form opens into a slow current of silver and dark blue. An Var lowers her hands when there are no longer hands to hold.

The current widens through the grass and air. It passes into the light on the water.

Chen reaches for Olga's hand. She holds it.

Time passes in the valley. Shadows lengthen. The moons rise. Colours move across the gathering: blue and gold, orange and green.

The space where Quirong stood is quiet.

Then the grass bends inward. Light gathers close to the ground, rising gradually into a figure.

## Sequence 7 — Str So Fle

A being steps forward.

Quirong's height remains recognisable. The form is organic, its shoulders deep blue, its hands warm silver. Dark hair lifts in the wind.

Their eyes open. They look at their hands, turning them slowly.

Peijin makes a small sound between laughter and tears.

**CHEN**
Quirong?

The being turns towards him.

**STR SO FLE**
I am Str So Fle. But yes. I remember being Quirong. I remember coming here with you. All of you.

Olga comes a little closer.

**OLGA**
Are you all right?

Str So Fle laughs. The sound surprises them. They pause, then laugh again, openly delighted.

**STR SO FLE**
All right? I am.

They lift their face into the wind.

**STR SO FLE**
I can feel it. The wind. The water. The grass. The planet is singing. It has always been singing. I just could not hear it before.

Ya Sha approaches. They reach for each other and embrace.

Str So Fle closes their eyes, then opens them to look at her again.

**STR SO FLE**
I want to stay.

Ya Sha smiles and holds them closer.

Around them, movement returns to the valley. Col An takes Sai Ya's hand. Peijin wipes her face and laughs. Nick sits in the grass, looking up at the being before him.

A wave reaches the shore. Its bright edge catches the colours of both moons.
