# Beautiful new world

  
Music reference: Beautiful New World / Home Sweet Home — Danny Elfman.

## Sequence 1 — More than my function

INT. HOUSE OF MEANINGFUL ENCOUNTERS — BEFORE DEPARTURE

Return to Olga's recorded encounter. Her navigation lights glow faintly beneath
her wrist.

**OLGA** I was given a body that can endure distances, and a mind that can hold
routes through them. I am a navigator. I am also more than my function.

**INTERVIEWER (O.S.)** When did you first know that?

**OLGA** I do not know. Perhaps I am still finding out.

CUT TO:

## Sequence 2 — A little different

EXT. XYZ — HIGH MEADOW — MORNING

The morning after the encounter with Kho Pa.

Olga follows An Var through silver-tipped grass. The sea lies far below. The
orange moon is sinking behind the hills; the blue one remains pale in the sky.

An Var's hair is longer. The freckles on her face have become small points of
gold.

Olga looks twice. An Var catches the second glance.

**AN VAR** You can ask.

**OLGA** Did you change your appearance?

**AN VAR** A little.

**OLGA** Why?

An Var looks towards the sinking moon.

**AN VAR** It felt right this morning.

A breeze crosses the meadow. Olga draws her coat closer.

**OLGA** Are you cold?

**AN VAR** No. We adapt to the chill. The wind. The rain.

Olga takes a drink from her flask, then offers it.

**AN VAR** Thank you. I do not need it. We do not eat or drink. Or sleep.

Olga lowers the flask, smiling with tired disbelief.

**OLGA** I still need all three.

**AN VAR** Then we will make room for all three.

They walk on at Olga's pace.

## Sequence 3 — A place where we make

EXT. XYZ — MEADOW HOLLOW — CONTINUOUS

Grass forms a circle around a shallow hollow filled with blue light. The
radiance rises and falls gently.

**OLGA** What is this?

**AN VAR** A place where we make. Sometimes we come here to meditate.

**OLGA** What do you meditate on?

**AN VAR** What we want to create. We do not meditate to become conscious. We are
already conscious.

She reaches into the hollow. Light gathers around her fingers.

**AN VAR** The planet gives us power. The moons give us power. Sometimes other
planets too, if everyone agrees and it is needed.

**OLGA** On Earth, we use energy for almost everything. Travel. Healing. Changing
bodies. Making homes.

**AN VAR** That is beautiful.

**OLGA** You say that about everything Earth does.

**AN VAR** Not everything.

Olga laughs. An Var sits and pats the grass beside her.

Small blue insects cross the light. Olga watches them as she settles.

## Sequence 4 — Before you could answer

**AN VAR** How were you made?

Olga's smile fades into thought.

**OLGA** I was grown. My lungs, muscles, bones, and nervous system were adapted
for travel. My navigation systems were integrated before I woke.

**AN VAR** Did you choose it?

**OLGA** No.

The gold in An Var's freckles softens.

**OLGA** But I was cared for. I was wanted. I have never lacked love. I chose my
work after I was awake. I chose this mission.

**AN VAR** I am not saying you were not loved.

Olga waits.

**AN VAR** On XYZ, we decide together to make a new being. Those who will care
for them, the planet, and the being themself. We ask. We listen. They begin to
answer while they are still growing.

**OLGA** You speak with them before they are born?

**AN VAR** For months, sometimes longer.

**OLGA** And they can refuse?

**AN VAR** They can wait. Choose another way. Choose not to come yet.

Olga looks down at the navigation lights in her wrist. She turns her hand
slowly, as though seeing something she has overlooked.

**AN VAR** You did not get to participate in your own beginning. I am sorry.

Olga becomes very still.

An insect settles on a blade of grass between them. The light continues its
slow movement.

Olga takes a long breath. Tears come. An Var remains beside her without
reaching across the space.

At last, Olga offers her hand. An Var takes it.

**OLGA** I have been happy.

An Var listens.

**OLGA** And I am sad.

**AN VAR** Both can be true.

Hold with them. Let the silence last beyond the exchange.

## Sequence 5 — The House of Knowing

EXT. XYZ — HOUSE OF KNOWING — LATER

An open structure of pale stone and living wood. Branches pass through spaces
where walls might have been.

Olga and An Var approach. Under a spreading tree, several beings move through a
slow choreoyoga pattern. A missed turn becomes laughter; the movement continues.

Olga recognises the gesture of two raised hands and answers it almost
unconsciously. One of the dancers smiles at her.

INT. HOUSE OF KNOWING — CONTINUOUS

A four-armed being arranges translucent leaves. Nearby, a winged being hangs
comfortably from a branch. Light crosses blue, gold, brown, and silver forms.

A RAM sits among them, silver hair resting over one shoulder. They look up as
An Var approaches. She sits beside them with easy familiarity.

**AN VAR** This is A Ram. This is Olga, our navigator.

A Ram gestures to the space beside them. Olga sits at the same level.

**A RAM** Then you understand that arriving somewhere changes the route that
brought you.

Olga looks at An Var.

**OLGA** I am beginning to.

**A RAM** Our knowledge is in the conscious energy space. Anyone may ask. No one
owns it.

**OLGA** And nothing is lost?

**A RAM** Sometimes we change so much that we do not recognise one another.
Sometimes someone travels far and chooses another home. But the energy of a
being does not vanish.

A being passing nearby pauses to touch A Ram's offered hand. They exchange a
smile before the visitor continues.

**A RAM** When we recognise an old friend, we rejoice. When we do not, we rejoice
in meeting someone new.

**OLGA** So death is not an ending?

**A RAM** Death is a word for a change you cannot yet follow.

Olga looks towards the open sky.

**OLGA** On Earth, we still need devices for so many of these things.

**A RAM** Then your devices are part of how Earth loves. There is no shame in
that.

Olga's shoulders relax. She rests her hands openly in her lap.

## Sequence 6 — From Earth

An Var looks from Olga to A Ram.

**AN VAR** Tell her where we came from.

A Ram gives Olga a moment of quiet attention.

**A RAM** From Earth.

Olga stops moving.

The leaves rustle overhead. Somewhere outside, the dancers laugh again.

**OLGA** Earth?

**A RAM** Long ago, a group of Earth beings stopped eating, drinking, and
sleeping. They called themselves autonomous. They disappeared because they
changed beyond what Earth then knew how to see. They became us.

Olga looks around the room: the wings, the many hands, the colours moving
across familiar and unfamiliar faces.

**OLGA** You are human?

**A RAM** We are what humans can become. And you can become this, too. Some of
you already did.

Olga looks back at An Var. An Var smiles, letting the words settle.

EXT. HOUSE OF KNOWING — LATER

Olga pauses beneath the open branches. The blue moon shines faintly in the
daylight.

She lays one hand over the navigation lights beneath her wrist. Then lifts her
eyes towards the meadow and the sea beyond it.

An Var waits beside the path. Olga joins her.
