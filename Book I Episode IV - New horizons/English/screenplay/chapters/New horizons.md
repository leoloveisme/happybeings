# New horizons

Background music: to be decided. Allow the landing's mechanical sounds to give way to wind, water, and the forest.

## Sequence 1 — The invitation becomes a place

EXT. XYZ — ABOVE THE LANDING SHORE

SECOND HORIZON descends through pearl-grey clouds.

Below: a broad curve of sand between forest and clear ocean. Dark green shapes move far beneath the water. The blue and orange moons hang above the bay.

No landing tower. No guiding lights. The ship settles at the coordinates it was given.

INT. SECOND HORIZON — NAVIGATION CHAMBER

The engine note falls away.

For a few breaths, everyone remains still.

**NICK**
Atmosphere stable. No conventional power grid. No communication source I can isolate.

**CHEN**
No danger?

Nick looks through the glass.

**NICK**
That is not what I said.

Quirong waits beside the door, hands loosely folded.

**QUIRONG**
The invitation remains active. I recommend we accept it.

Peijin rests a palm against the wall.

**PEIJIN**
I cannot feel danger. I can feel... attention. And curiosity.

She opens her eyes.

Nick leaves the remote scanners in their rack and takes his small engineering case. Peijin lifts her medical pack.

Olga touches the inner wall beside the hatch. Her hand remains there until the ramp begins to open.

## Sequence 2 — Warm air

EXT. XYZ — LANDING SHORE — CONTINUOUS

Wind enters the ship before the crew steps out.

Olga pauses at the top of the ramp, breathing in. Grass bends in silver lines along the edge of the sand.

The forest sounds around them: small clicking calls, low sustained tones, brief notes almost like laughter.

The crew descends. Quirong comes with them.

People emerge between the trees.

One has four arms, the upper pair lifted into the wind. Another's skin shades from yellow at the throat to blue at the shoulders. A tall being folds narrow wings while stepping onto the beach. Their unclothed forms move easily through the warm evening air.

Nick looks from them to the forest, searching for the structures his instruments could not find. Then he meets a welcoming gaze and lets the engineering case rest beside his leg.

AN VAR steps forward. She has the appearance of an Earth child of about nine, a loose dark bob, freckles, and an open smile. Soft peach and gold colours shift across her face in the moonlight.

She stops several steps from the crew, leaving space between them.

**AN VAR**
Welcome.

Earth words. Chen looks briefly at Olga, then smiles back.

**CHEN**
Thank you.

**AN VAR**
You are the ones who came because you could not find the others.

**CHEN**
Yes. I am Chen. This is Olga, Peijin, Nick, and Quirong.

An Var acknowledges each in turn.

**AN VAR**
I am An Var.

## Sequence 3 — Speaking and knowing

Behind An Var, a glance passes between two beings. Several smile at once. No words are spoken.

Nick turns quietly to Olga.

**NICK**
Did the ship translate that?

**OLGA**
No. She is speaking our language.

**CHEN**
Peijin? Are you all right?

**PEIJIN**
Yes. Just listening.

An Var has followed their exchange with increasing interest.

**AN VAR**
Why do you ask each other questions instead of just knowing?

Nick looks at Olga again, then catches himself doing it.

Peijin smiles a little.

**PEIJIN**
We do not know any other way. We speak. We listen. We try to understand.

An Var considers this.

**AN VAR**
That sounds beautiful. And tiring.

Chen takes a breath, searching for an answer. An Var waits without impatience.

**NICK**
How old are you?

**AN VAR**
Two hundred.

His mouth opens slightly. Peijin turns towards An Var with renewed attention.

**AN VAR**
I am just finishing childhood.

A gentle laugh passes through the beings behind her. An Var smiles with them.

Quirong looks at Nick, then back at An Var.

## Sequence 4 — Safe

Chen takes a small step forward.

**CHEN**
An Var, may I ask about the first expedition?

Her attention settles fully on him.

**AN VAR**
Yes. You may ask.

**CHEN**
Where are they?

A low note carries from the forest. Grass moves in the wind.

**AN VAR**
They are safe.

**PEIJIN**
Are they alive?

**AN VAR**
They are sleeping.

Quirong's folded hands separate.

**CHEN**
Can we see them?

An Var looks towards the others. They exchange a quiet attention, faces turning, eyes meeting. The crew waits through a conversation it cannot hear.

An Var turns back.

**AN VAR**
Not yet.

Nick becomes very still.

**NICK**
You are keeping them from us.

**AN VAR**
Our collective mind hid the ship and all its crew. We are keeping them safe.

**CHEN**
Why were they hidden?

## Sequence 5 — What exploration touched

An Var kneels and places her hand on the sand. A few grains lift around her fingers, then settle in a loose spiral.

**AN VAR**
They watched us before they knew us. They drilled into our planet. Scooped our water. Captured our air.

She looks up.

**AN VAR**
Our bodies.

Peijin looks at the water, then the trees. Nick's hand loosens on his case.

**AN VAR**
They did not mean to hurt us. They thought it was only exploration.

**CHEN**
And you stopped them.

**AN VAR**
We made them sleep. We hid the ship and held it safely. We wanted the exploration to stop.

Quirong steps closer, stopping beside Chen.

**QUIRONG**
They have friends waiting for them.

**AN VAR**
Yes.

She looks directly at Quirong as she answers.

**NICK**
You could have told Earth.

An Var rises.

**AN VAR**
We did not know how. We had never met beings who needed to speak like this. With time between one knowing and another.

Nick glances towards Second Horizon.

**NICK**
But you called us down.

**AN VAR**
This time, we found a way to invite you.

Nick holds her gaze. He looks as though he has another question, but lets the answer stand for a moment.

## Sequence 6 — Here

**CHEN**
Will you take us to them?

**AN VAR**
Later. I will take you there later.

**PEIJIN**
It is hard to wait when someone you care about is missing.

An Var looks across the crew, pausing at Quirong.

**AN VAR**
First you have to be here. Not above us. Not outside us. Here.

Chen looks at the sand under his feet. Then at An Var.

**CHEN**
I can do that.

**AN VAR**
Good.

She holds out her hands in welcome, then turns towards the path. The others make room for the visitors.

**AN VAR**
Come. There is so much you have not observed yet.

Olga catches the word. A small, uncertain smile.

Chen looks back once at Second Horizon. Its white hull stands against the living shore.

He follows An Var. Peijin walks beside him. Nick takes up his case and joins them. Quirong remains a moment, looking towards the forest, then follows with Olga.

EXT. XYZ — FOREST PATH — CONTINUOUS

The sea is still audible behind them. Ahead, the sounds of the trees grow fuller.

An Var slows to match the visitors' pace.
