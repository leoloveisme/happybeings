# Vast vast space

Background music reference: The XX Intro by Laurent Zen.

## Sequence 1 — A way through

EXT. SPACE

SECOND HORIZON moves through the starfield. Earth is lost behind it.

The music opens into the distance. Hold the ship small in the frame.

**INTERVIEWER (V.O.)**
Would you tell me your name?

**OLGA (V.O.)**
Olga Ahlam Pelin-Lurie.

INT. HOUSE OF MEANINGFUL ENCOUNTERS — BEFORE DEPARTURE

Olga sits in the morning light. The interviewer remains outside the frame.

**INTERVIEWER (O.S.)**
And how do you describe yourself?

**OLGA**
I am a navigator. I was created for long journeys and adapted for space travel before I was awake enough to agree to anything.

She considers how that sounds.

**OLGA**
That used to sound sad when other people said it. It does not always feel sad to me. I love space. I love distance. I love finding a way through something so large that it does not seem to have a way through it.

**INTERVIEWER (O.S.)**
Did you choose this mission?

**OLGA**
Yes. That is important. I was made with capacities. I choose what to do with them.

**INTERVIEWER (O.S.)**
What do you expect to find at XYZ?

**OLGA**
A ship that is still there, and beings who may still be waiting. I hope they know we have not forgotten them.

CUT TO:

## Sequence 2 — Twelve days

INT. SECOND HORIZON — NAVIGATION CHAMBER

Routes unfold in light around Olga. Her wrist lights pulse as she follows a curve through the field. A route changes; she watches it settle before moving her hand.

A quiet passage of days:

— Chen stands at the viewing glass. Stars reflect across his face. Later, he sits in the same place, a cup cooling beside him.

— Peijin stretches beside the glass, then settles into stillness. The ship's interior light dims towards its night cycle.

— Nick listens beside an open maintenance panel, checks a reading, and closes it. He stays another moment, listening.

— Quirong records another call to First Horizon. The reply field remains empty.

— At the common table, hands pass food. A chair changes occupants. A meal becomes another meal.

— Olga's route field contracts. The remaining distance grows shorter.

On the navigation display: JOURNEY DAY 12.

The music recedes beneath the ship's soft interior sound.

## Sequence 3 — Two moons

INT. SECOND HORIZON — NAVIGATION CHAMBER

Chen enters. Olga makes room beside her without taking her eyes from the field.

**OLGA**
There.

A point of colour enlarges into XYZ: deep blue-green seas, forests, pale mountain ranges. Clouds cross the boundary between daylight and night.

Two moons hang beside it. One clear blue. One warm orange.

Let the image remain before either speaks.

**CHEN**
It looks like Earth.

**OLGA**
It looks like a planet. Earth does not own that shape.

Chen laughs softly.

**CHEN**
You are right. It is more colourful than Earth.

A small smile from Olga.

**OLGA**
That is allowed.

## Sequence 4 — The waiting

INT. SECOND HORIZON — COMMON AREA — SHIP'S EVENING

XYZ fills part of the viewing glass. Dinner is on the table. Very little has been eaten.

Peijin sets down her spoon.

**PEIJIN**
Do you think the people there are watching us?

Nick looks up from a diagnostic display.

**NICK**
If they have orbital monitoring, then yes.

**PEIJIN**
That is not what I meant.

He lowers the display and looks towards the planet.

**NICK**
Yes. I think they might be.

Quirong stands at the viewing glass.

**QUIRONG**
The first expedition will have been visible from the surface as well.

Silence around the table.

**CHEN**
Play their message again.

Quirong brings up the archived transmission. A plain label identifies FIRST HORIZON — FINAL RECEIVED REPORT.

**FIRST EXPEDITION CREW MEMBER (RECORDED)**
Landing successful. Atmosphere breathable. Gravity close to Earth's. Conditions favourable for life.

The recording ends. The ship's low hum continues.

**NICK**
One message. No distress call. Then no trace of the ship.

**PEIJIN**
We should have sent someone sooner.

**CHEN**
The journey takes time.

**PEIJIN**
That is true. It does not make the waiting less real.

Quirong turns towards them.

**QUIRONG**
The first crew had agreed to autonomous mission protocols. They understood that direct assistance could not arrive quickly.

**NICK**
That does not mean they agreed to be abandoned.

**QUIRONG**
No. It does not.

Olga draws the unfinished bowl closer to her. For a moment she simply holds it.

Beyond the glass, a line of sunlight moves across XYZ.

## Sequence 5 — No trace

INT. SECOND HORIZON — NAVIGATION CHAMBER — SHIP'S MORNING

Olga at navigation. Nick at engineering. Quirong linked to the passive sensors. Chen and Peijin watch the planet below.

The search field returns no match for First Horizon.

Nick checks the sensor status. Runs the comparison again. Checks it a third time.

**NICK**
No debris. No energy signature. Nothing from the landing site.

**PEIJIN**
Could they have left?

**NICK**
We have no trace of that either.

Olga studies the field.

**OLGA**
It is hidden.

**CHEN**
Hidden by what?

**OLGA**
I do not know.

**NICK**
We cannot tell that from an empty reading.

Olga looks at him, then back at the planet.

**OLGA**
No. We cannot. But I think they are still here.

Below: open shapes among trees, paths curving towards a coast, small lights beside a river.

Chen leans closer. Olga holds the broad view.

**CHEN**
Somebody lives here.

Quirong opens a channel.

**QUIRONG**
First Horizon, this is Quirong of Second Horizon. We have arrived for assistance. Can you receive us?

They wait.

## Sequence 6 — An open shore

A pattern of tones sounds through the chamber.

Quirong turns to the console. The pattern repeats, warm and clear. A location appears on the planetary map: an open stretch of shore.

**QUIRONG**
This is not a code from the first expedition.

Olga listens. Her navigation lights shift from white to soft blue.

**OLGA**
That looks like an invitation.

Nick studies the coordinates.

**NICK**
A place to land. We do not know who sent it.

**CHEN**
Can we answer?

Quirong opens the channel again.

**QUIRONG**
We are Second Horizon, from Earth. We are looking for our missing friends. We have received your coordinates. May we approach?

The tones repeat. The same shore remains marked.

Olga lays a proposed descent path beside the coordinates and waits.

**CHEN**
Slowly. No drones. Only what we need to bring the ship down safely. If they ask us to leave, we leave.

**PEIJIN**
Agreed.

Nick looks from the shore to Quirong, then closes the search display.

**NICK**
Agreed.

Quirong nods. Olga brings the route into alignment.

EXT. XYZ — ORBIT

Second Horizon turns towards the planet. Cloud crosses beneath its hull.

The orange moon rises beyond the curve of the world.

DISSOLVE TO:

EXT. XYZ — SHORE — EVENING

The same orange moon above a distant headland. Waves move over the sand.

Stay with the shore as the sound of water fills the scene.

CONTINUE INTO: ENJOYING BEING.
