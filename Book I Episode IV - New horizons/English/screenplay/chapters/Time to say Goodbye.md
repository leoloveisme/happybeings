# Time to say "Goodbye"

## Sequence 1 — The last morning

EXT. XYZ — SECOND HORIZON LANDING SHORE — MORNING

Both moons remain visible. The blue one hangs above the western hills; the orange one pales towards yellow over the sea.

Near SECOND HORIZON, Nick makes room inside the boarding hatch while Peijin walks with a cyborg from the first expedition. Robots and androids wait together, talking quietly with their XYZ hosts.

One of the returning beings pauses to look back at the forest. Peijin waits beside them until they are ready to continue.

Str So Fle stands with Ya Sha at the edge of the gathering, their fingers threaded together. A breeze lifts their dark hair. They turn towards it and smile.

Chen approaches A Ram at the waterline.

**CHEN**
I did not expect the goodbye to feel like this.

**A RAM**
Like what?

**CHEN**
Like leaving home and finding home at the same time.

**A RAM**
Earth and XYZ are relatives.

**CHEN**
Olga told us where you came from. The autonomous people. On Earth, the story ends with their disappearance.

**A RAM**
Some stories became myths. Some myths became forgotten.

Chen watches the water reach his feet.

**CHEN**
They did not disappear.

**A RAM**
No. They arrived.

Chen laughs quietly, eyes wet.

## Sequence 2 — I want to go with you

EXT. XYZ — BESIDE SECOND HORIZON — CONTINUOUS

Olga closes a small travel bag. An Var sits beside her, watching the preparations.

**AN VAR**
Are you ready to go home?

Olga looks from the bag to the shore.

**OLGA**
I think so.

An Var looks towards the forest, then the ship.

**AN VAR**
I want to go with you.

Olga turns and kneels beside her.

**OLGA**
To Earth?

**AN VAR**
Yes.

**OLGA**
Why?

**AN VAR**
I am finishing childhood. It is time for youth. Trying. Experimenting.

Olga studies her face.

**AN VAR**
I want to know what it feels like to find harmony instead of simply living in it.

**OLGA**
Sometimes it feels like a very long meeting.

An Var laughs. Olga smiles with her, then grows thoughtful again.

**OLGA**
Who do we need to ask?

**AN VAR**
Each other.

She stands and offers Olga her hand.

## Sequence 3 — Making room

EXT. XYZ — OPEN CIRCLE OF STONE — MORNING

The Earth crew joins An Var, A Ram, and the gathered XYZ beings. The circle is open towards the sea.

An Var speaks aloud for the visitors.

**AN VAR**
I want to go to Earth. I want to learn from beings who speak to know one another. To see their cities, their Houses of meaningful encounters, their water, their dances, their ways of remembering.

She looks towards Olga and the crew.

**AN VAR**
I want to go with them.

No hands rise. The XYZ beings give their attention. A few exchange looks; others remain still. The sound of the receding tide fills the silence.

An Var closes her eyes. A smile grows across her face.

**AN VAR**
They say yes.

Nick looks at Olga, then Chen. Both nod.

**NICK**
We will need to prepare. Somewhere for you to stay. People you can turn to. Time.

**AN VAR**
Then we can prepare.

**PEIJIN**
And if you want to come home?

**AN VAR**
I can come home.

Peijin smiles.

**PEIJIN**
Good.

Olga holds out her hand again. An Var takes it.

## Sequence 4 — I wanted to say it myself

EXT. XYZ — LANDING SHORE — LATER

Str So Fle approaches the Earth crew. Ya Sha walks beside them.

**STR SO FLE**
I am staying.

**CHEN**
We know.

**STR SO FLE**
I wanted to say it myself.

Chen nods, giving them his full attention.

**STR SO FLE**
I want to learn this life. And I love Ya Sha.

Ya Sha turns towards them.

**YA SHA**
And I love you.

Str So Fle looks at each of their travelling companions.

**STR SO FLE**
I remember bringing you here. Thank you for bringing me here.

They open their arms to Chen. He steps into the embrace and laughs through tears.

**CHEN**
You are very soft.

**STR SO FLE**
I wanted to be.

They embrace Olga, then Peijin. Nick holds on longest. Str So Fle gives him the time.

The robot who recognised Quirong aboard First Horizon approaches. Str So Fle offers both hands, as before. Their old companion takes them, looking at the silver fingers with quiet wonder.

**STR SO FLE**
Give my love to our friends.

The robot nods and holds their hands a moment longer.

## Sequence 5 — Something to carry

Col An approaches Chen.

**COL AN**
May I share something with you? A memory.

**CHEN**
Yes.

Col An offers his hands. Chen takes them.

A brief impression: feet running over wet sand, wind, two paths of moonlight across the sea.

BACK ON THE SHORE

Chen takes a surprised breath, then smiles.

Nearby, Sai Ya faces Peijin.

**SAI YA**
Would you like to feel the sea as I remember it?

**PEIJIN**
I would.

They bring their foreheads gently together. Peijin closes her eyes. The sound of water surrounds her for a moment.

A Ram sits beside Olga.

**A RAM**
May I share a feeling for your journey?

**OLGA**
Yes.

Their hands meet. Olga looks down at her wrist lights and smiles without hiding them beneath her sleeve.

Ya Sha offers a hand to Nick.

**YA SHA**
Would you like to experience a little of how we are connected?

Nick looks at her hand. This time, he takes it.

**NICK**
Yes. Just for a moment.

The wind moves through the gathering. Nick's expression changes. His grip relaxes; he lets out a long breath.

Ya Sha releases his hand when he opens his eyes.

**NICK**
Thank you.

An Var walks between the groups, sharing embraces and silent farewells with her friends. At the edge of the sand she stops, closes her eyes, and rests both palms on the ground.

Then she rises and returns to Olga.

## Sequence 6 — Every possible way

EXT. XYZ — SECOND HORIZON RAMP — LATER

The ramp is open. Nick waits at its foot while Peijin accompanies the first expedition aboard. Each returning being has time for a final look and a farewell.

Chen joins Olga and An Var.

From beside Ya Sha, Str So Fle surveys the gathering with an almost familiar stillness.

**STR SO FLE**
You are all still saying goodbye.

Nick looks back from the ramp.

**NICK**
That is not a fault.

**STR SO FLE**
I did not say it was.

Their laughter follows the crew towards the ship.

At the top of the ramp, An Var turns. The wind lifts her hair. She looks from her friends to the forest and the wide, bright sea.

**AN VAR**
Goodbye.

A pause. Then she laughs.

**AN VAR**
Or: see you in every possible way.

She steps inside with Olga. Chen follows. Nick takes one last look towards Str So Fle and Ya Sha, then joins the others.

The ramp closes.

Str So Fle and Ya Sha remain together on the shore beneath the two moons. Around them, their friends look towards the waiting ship.
