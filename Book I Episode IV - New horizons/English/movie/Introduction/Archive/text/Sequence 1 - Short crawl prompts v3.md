# Opening crawl — Short generation prompts, version 3

Based on scroll text.mp4: approximately 15 seconds per clip. This version replaces the ten text sections in v2 with six. V2 remains available.

## Seven prompts total

- Prompt 0: title, 15 seconds.
- Prompts 1–6: six narration/text clips, 15 seconds each.
- Nominal assembled duration: 105 seconds before trimming or adding a short black opening and final starfield hold.

All 165 narration words are preserved in their original order. Sentence boundaries are retained, so the sections vary in length. The longer sections need a slightly quicker reading; the final section leaves space for the missing friends and the decision to go looking. Record Bee first and adjust final timing to his natural delivery. If a section feels rushed, extend it in the edit rather than speeding up his voice.

## What needs correcting from the current video

The test shows flat upward scrolling and distorted or duplicated lettering. The prompts below specify a tilted text plane, converging edges, and continuous shrinking towards a vanishing point. Splitting the text helps pacing but does not guarantee accurate generated lettering.

Generate text clip 1 first. Use the same approved starfield reference and identical settings throughout. Keep Bee's voice and music as continuous separate tracks. These separate paragraph clips will have paragraph gaps; a seamless continuous crawl and guaranteed spelling require editable perspective text in the editor.

## Prompt 0 — Title (15 seconds)

> A fixed black starfield, sparse fine stars. Two lines of crisp warm-gold bold lettering, exactly: HAPPY BEINGS / EPISODE IV — NEW HORIZONS. Centre the two lines as one title group, with the first line larger. Hold the readable title briefly, then shrink the entire group smoothly towards a distant point in the upper centre until it disappears into the stars. Lettering remains exact and stable. The camera and starfield are stationary. One uninterrupted shot, silent, widescreen 16:9. Only the two specified title lines are visible; the slash indicates a line break and is not printed.

## Prompt 1 — Text clip 01 (27 words)

Create a 15-second cinematic perspective text crawl over a fixed black starfield. The lettering lies flat on a single imaginary plane tilted away from the viewer into deep space. At the bottom of the frame the text is large and wide; farther up it becomes progressively smaller and narrower. The left and right edges of the text column converge towards one vanishing point at horizontal centre, 20 percent below the top of the frame. The whole paragraph travels smoothly along this tilted plane, from below the bottom edge towards that distant point, shrinking continuously as it recedes. This is the spatial geometry of the classic Star Wars opening crawl. The camera and stars remain stationary. Use crisp warm-gold bold lettering, normal sentence case, generous line spacing, and the exact words supplied below. The letters retain their shape and spelling throughout. Let the text enter immediately from the lower edge, remain readable through the central portion of the shot, then recede towards the vanishing point. Use the full clip for the text movement, with no long empty opening or ending. A single paragraph appears once. Silent video; voice and music will be added separately. Widescreen 16:9.

EXACT ON-SCREEN TEXT:
On Earth, we stopped using money so long ago that most of us have forgotten it. I saw some once, in a history lesson. Little metal circles.

## Joining the results

- Keep one continuous Bee voice performance and one music bed across the sequence.
- Trim empty opening and ending frames to produce deliberate paragraph pauses.
- Do not crossfade overlapping text: that doubles the letters.
- Check each complete clip, not only its opening frame, before accepting it.
- If the first short clip still corrupts words, stop generating lettering and use the editor-based crawl in the original prompt package.
